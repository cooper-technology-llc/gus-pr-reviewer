# Review invariants

- INV-REVIEW-1: A published finding cites host-recorded evidence at pinned revision coordinates; removed-line findings also cite a current consequence — enforced by `logic/adjudicate-review.ts#validateAnalysisEvidence` and `logic/review-evidence.ts#addEvidence`.
- INV-REVIEW-2: Validation accounts for every investigation candidate and unresolved prior finding; unavailable current proof is recorded as an unverified candidate or reconciliation rather than copied blockers, and never withholds the verdict — enforced by `logic/adjudicate-review.ts#validateCandidateResolutions`, `logic/adjudicate-review.ts#validateReconciliations`, and `review-change.ts#applyValidatedAssessment`.
- INV-REVIEW-3: Findings meeting the configured blocking severity cannot become nonblocking through their disposition field — enforced by `logic/adjudicate-review.ts#isBlocking` and `logic/adjudicate-review.ts#normalizeFindingIds`.
- INV-REVIEW-4: Every stage and repository call shares a finite time budget; investigate/validate context overflow or an invalid tool-phase result rebuilds a compact no-tool submit instead of discarding collected evidence, and that submit's checks (grammar, schema, evidence-ID existence, candidate accounting) are all satisfiable without tools; only its failure stops the review incomplete with host-owned coverage — enforced by `review-budget.ts#ReviewBudget`, `structured-stage.ts#runStructuredStage`, `structured-stage.ts#runCompactSubmit`, and `review-change.ts#reviewChange`.
- INV-REVIEW-5: The verdict is incomplete only when no validated assessment exists (a required stage failed, or the change exceeded maxFiles or the prior-finding limit); required-stage failure removes grades; otherwise the verdict is changes-requested exactly when a blocking finding exists or a supplied check on this head failed; optional prose cannot mutate findings, grades, or verdict — enforced by `review-change.ts#reviewChange`, `review-change.ts#applyValidatedAssessment`, `review-change.ts#skippedReview`, and `review-change.ts#addPersonality`.
- INV-REVIEW-6: Provider credentials are sent only to configured HTTPS or loopback HTTP endpoints, without credential-bearing URL fields or redirect forwarding — enforced by `../model/model-client.ts#providerEndpoint` and `../model/model-client.ts#createModelClient`.
- INV-REVIEW-7: Configured rules inspect literal added text and changed companion paths independently, expose no excluded source text, and produce advisory signals rather than findings or verdicts — enforced by `logic/configured-rule-signals.ts#configuredRuleSignals` and `review-seed.ts#buildReviewSeed`.
- INV-REVIEW-8: Informational omissions, tool warnings, and optional voice failures stay in saved diagnostics; coverage (including partial, unreviewed, and not-applicable binaries and pure renames) lives only in `coverage`/`coverageSummary` and questions only in `questions`; `limitations` holds only real limitations (tool failures, unverified candidates, deadline or provider errors, integration conflict or unavailability, checks on another head) and never copies coverage rows — enforced by `review-change.ts#assessmentLimitations`, `review-change.ts#reviewChange`, `review-change.ts#addPersonality`, `logic/adjudicate-review.ts#buildCoverage`, `logic/adjudicate-review.ts#summarizeCoverage`, and `structured-stage.ts#inspectWithTool`.
- INV-REVIEW-9: Non-triage stages author complete text DSL documents without provider JSON constraints; local parsing preserves prose while rejecting malformed records before the existing schema and evidence checks can accept an assessment — enforced by `logic/review-dsl-lexer.ts#readReviewDsl`, `logic/review-dsl-parser.ts#parseReviewDsl`, and `structured-stage.ts#runStructuredStage`.
- INV-REVIEW-10: Per-call usage contains only scalar accounting metadata and permitted tool names; missing provider accounting remains unknown in call records and stage totals — enforced by `review-budget.ts#ReviewBudget.finishModelCall` and `../reporting/logic/format-model-usage.ts#formatModelUsage`.
- INV-REVIEW-11: Model context reuses exact source strings only through definitions available in the same stage, while retaining every evidence identity and the host's complete original evidence for validation — enforced by `logic/review-context.ts#createReviewContext`, `structured-stage.ts#runStructuredStage`, and `logic/review-evidence.ts#addEvidence`.
- INV-REVIEW-12: The host owns coverage and evidence text: before triage it pages every truncated reviewable patch through the repository executor (bounded by a byte budget), and after validation it re-reads each cited evidence range at its pinned revision and attaches at most 12 lines as a finding excerpt; these reads never enter the model conversation, never count against maxToolCalls, and a failed read leaves a notice rather than failing the review — enforced by `host-repository-reads.ts#prefetchTruncatedPatches`, `host-repository-reads.ts#attachFindingExcerpts`, and `logic/adjudicate-review.ts#normalizeFindingIds`.
